# CRS math: fallback area of use, CRS units, antimeridian

## Problem

- The wroge/crs fallback returned +Inf for every point outside the EPSG area of use or
  outside the datum transformation areas. All four corners of the standard EEA grid in
  EPSG:3035 (CORINE, EU-DEM) failed, so the merged bounds became lon [180, +Inf], the
  zoom range 0–0 and the output one empty tile, with no error. A 3035 raster of Iceland or
  the Canaries rendered transparent.
- `PixelSizeInGroundMeters` and `MetersToPixelSizeCRS` treated every CRS but 4326 and 3857
  as meters. A 1/3″ DEM in EPSG:4269 got an auto max zoom of 28 (13 is right) and always
  read the coarsest overview; State Plane rasters in US survey feet got a max zoom about
  two levels too low.
- Longitudes were never wrapped. A 0..360 grid got bounds 0..360, which clamped to the last
  tile column, and the western hemisphere was never generated. UTM zones 60 and 1 lost
  everything past 180°.

## Changes

- `internal/coord/fallback.go`: the projection runs with an unrestricted area of use
  (lon ±540 so points across the antimeridian from the central meridian pass). `ToWGS84`
  is now unproject + the cached datum shift instead of the exact, area-gated transform.
  Shift-grid nodes that no transformation covers take the shift at the centre of the
  CRS's area of use (~0 for ETRS89/NAD83/GDA94, ~150 m for ED50) instead of +Inf; zero
  if even that fails, like PROJ's ballpark transformation.
- `internal/coord/projection.go`: `unitSize(epsg)` reads the unit from the EPSG
  definition in wroge/crs. `PixelSizeInGroundMeters` and `MetersToPixelSizeCRS` use the
  degree formula for every geographic CRS (grads scaled) and convert feet
  (0.3048) and US survey feet (1200/3937) to meters.
- `internal/coord/projection.go`: `WrapLonRange` shifts a longitude range by whole
  turns so that MinLon is in [-180, 180). MaxLon > 180 marks bounds that cross the
  antimeridian, and a full turn becomes [-180, 180]. `WGS84Identity.Lon360` makes
  `FromWGS84` return western longitudes as lon+360, for 0..360 grids.
- `internal/coord/mercator.go`: `TilesInBounds` continues past the antimeridian from
  column 0 when MaxLon > 180. `MinZoomForSingleTile` returns 0 for such bounds.
- `internal/cog/reader.go` (`MergedBoundsWGS84`): skips corners that do not transform,
  panics if none does, and wraps the longitude range.
- Tests: EEA grid corners, Canaries and NSIDC corners round-trip; ED50 off its Helmert
  areas still gets a shift; units for 4269/4258/4807/2263; `WrapLonRange`, `Lon360`,
  antimeridian tile columns; merged bounds for 3035, UTM 60/1 and 0..360 grids.

## Not changed here

- `tile.Generate` must set `Lon360` for 0..360 sources, or the wrapped columns render
  empty. The PMTiles header should also write -180..180 when MaxLon > 180.
- Bounds still come from the four corners only. Curved edges and polar rasters are
  under-covered.
